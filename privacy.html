Privacy policy

Swarm · Last updated November 15, 2026

Swarm is a personal AI assistant used only by its owner. This policy explains what Swarm does with Google user data when the owner connects their Google account.

What Swarm accesses

With the owner's explicit permission, Swarm requests read-only access to:

  • Google Calendar: to answer questions about the owner's schedule.
  • Gmail: to find and summarise the owner's messages when asked.
  • Google Drive: to find and read the owner's documents when asked.

Swarm cannot send email, change calendar events, or edit or delete files.

How it's used

Google data is used only to answer the owner's own requests. It is processed on computers the owner controls. When a request needs it, a small part of that data may be sent to a cloud AI model the owner has configured, solely to produce the answer to that request.

What Swarm doesn't do

  • It does not sell, rent or share Google data with anyone.
  • It does not use Google data for advertising.
  • It does not use Google data to train AI models.

Swarm's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and deletion

Swarm stores only the sign-in tokens needed to keep access, on the owner's own computer. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, after which Swarm can no longer read any Google data.
