Swarm

A private, self-hosted AI assistant.

Swarm is a personal assistant that runs on its owner's own computers rather than in the cloud. It answers questions, helps with planning and code, and, with the owner's permission, reads their calendar, email and files so it can help with day-to-day tasks.

Who it's for

Swarm is used only by its owner. It isn't a public service and doesn't accept sign-ups.

Google data

When connected to a Google account, Swarm requests read-only access to Google Calendar, Gmail and Google Drive. It uses that access only to answer the owner's own questions. See the privacy policy for details.
